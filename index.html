How to play

Welcome to the club. Tilt the board — every marble slides at once until it hits something.

  • Swipe to tilt. Or use the arrow keys. Marbles roll all the way until a wall, a crate or another marble stops them.
  • Sink each marble in its own colour. Rolling over a hole drops the marble in, and the wrong colour costs you a life.
  • Avoid the drains. They swallow any marble. Crates slide too but never fall.
  • ★
    Go for three stars. Solve in par (the fewest moves possible) for ★★★, a little over par for ★★. Run out of moves and the board resets. Clear a level to unlock the next.
